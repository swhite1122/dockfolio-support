Dockfolio Support

iPhone-style app folders for the macOS Dock.

Do I need an account?

No. Dockfolio does not include accounts or sign-in.

What does Dockfolio do?

Dockfolio gives you up to eight folders, each with its own icon in the Dock. A folder icon shows a small preview of the apps inside it. Clicking the icon opens a panel next to the Dock so you can launch any app in that folder.

How do I add a folder to the Dock?

Open Dockfolio, create a folder, and add apps to it. The folder then appears as its own Dock icon. To keep it there permanently, right-click the icon in the Dock and choose Options, then Keep in Dock.

Why is eight the limit?

Each folder is a separate small program inside Dockfolio, and that is what allows it to have its own Dock icon rather than sharing one. Eight is the number Dockfolio ships with.

Do my folders come back after I restart?

Yes, if you turn on the option to start folders at login. Dockfolio uses Apple's standard login item system, so macOS may ask you to approve it the first time. You can turn it off at any time in Dockfolio, or in System Settings under General, then Login Items.

Why does each folder show up when I press Command-Tab?

macOS only gives a Dock icon to something that also appears in the app switcher. The two go together, and there is no way to have one without the other. This is a limitation of macOS, not a setting in Dockfolio.

Does Dockfolio need special permissions?

No. Dockfolio does not use the Accessibility permission, does not replace or modify the Dock itself, and does not change any of your system settings. It reads the list of apps installed on your Mac so it can show you a picker when you add an app to a folder.

Where is my data stored?

Your folders, their names, and the apps inside them are stored locally on your Mac.

Does Dockfolio use the internet, analytics, or ads?

No. Dockfolio contains no networking code at all. There is no backend, no analytics, no ads, and no cloud sync.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 15 or later.
