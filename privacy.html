Dockfolio Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Dockfolio is a local-only Mac app for organising apps into Dock folders. Everything it stores stays on your Mac.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, sell, or share user data from Dockfolio.

Dockfolio contains no networking code. It does not include accounts, backend servers, analytics, ads, iCloud sync, or third-party dependencies.

Your Folders

Folder names, the order of your folders, and the apps you place inside them are stored locally on your Mac. This information is never transmitted anywhere.

Installed Apps

So that it can show you a list to choose from, Dockfolio reads the names, icons, and locations of the apps installed on your Mac. This is read on your Mac, used only to draw the app picker and the folder previews, and is never sent off the device.

Login Items

If you choose to have your folders start automatically, Dockfolio registers itself with Apple's standard login item system so macOS can launch your folders when you log in. macOS controls this approval, and you can revoke it at any time in System Settings under General, then Login Items.

System Settings

Dockfolio does not read or change your macOS system settings, does not use the Accessibility permission, and does not modify or replace the Dock itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Dockfolio support repository.
